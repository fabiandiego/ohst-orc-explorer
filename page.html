Where is the pinch?

The paper's central result fits on one line: compare the heat source temperature ths with the fluid's OHST. Below the OHST the pinch sits at the evaporator inlet and heat is left unused. Above it the pinch moves to the preheater side and ηsys stops rising.

§4

Optimal Heat Source Temperature

At a fixed evaporation pressure, ηsys rises steeply with source temperature and then flattens. The bend is the OHST. There the preheater profiles run parallel, so the pinch touches the evaporator inlet and the preheater inlet together.

§5

Case study: thermal water at 140 °C

Evaporation pressure is the only optimisation variable. Fluids whose OHST lies above ths have an interior optimum (OEP); for the rest, the highest allowable pressure wins. A dual-fluid ORC then splits the source between a high- and a low-temperature loop.
